EVENT PROFILE

What CultureHacks Is

CultureHacks is the Santa Monica College Computer Science Club's build day: forty people, one room, twelve hours, and one organising question — what does a culture look like once you turn it into software?

We mean that in both senses. There is the culture you were raised in — language, food, faith, migration, the specific way your family argues about dinner. And there is the culture you were trained into — code review etiquette, terminal aesthetics, Bloomberg muscle memory, tabs versus spaces, a shared vocabulary that quietly marks who is inside and who is not.

Both are real cultures. Both have rituals, dialects, in-groups and unwritten rules. Both can be mapped, modelled, archived, translated — or hacked. Pick either. Pick both.

“Every engineering team is a culture with a dress code and a dialect. Every immigrant family is a distributed system with a consistency problem.”
  • 01

    Two Kinds of Culture

    Heritage and profession both count. A Tamil recipe archive and a tool that reads a repo's social hierarchy are equally on-theme.

  • 02

    You Need Curiosity, Not Credentials

    You do not have to be an expert in the culture you build about — but do the reading, and say where your material came from when you submit.

  • 03

    Small Room, On Purpose

    Forty people and twelve hours. Everybody demos, and nobody disappears into a crowd of four hundred.

COMPETITION STACK

Four Tracks, One Question

Pick one, or ignore all four and build the culture you actually care about.

TRACK 01 // LINEAGE

Heritage & Diaspora

Language preservation, recipe archives, migration mapping, religious and lunar calendars, translation that survives idiom. Anything that helps a culture stay legible to the next generation of it.

NLPArchivesTranslationMapping

TRACK 02 // THE TRIBE YOU JOINED

Engineering Culture

Commit archaeology, review etiquette, on-call rituals, the sociology of a monorepo. Software that studies the people who write software — ideally while making fun of them.

GitStatic AnalysisDev ToolsViz

TRACK 03 // THE TRIBE YOU JOINED

Markets & Quant Culture

Trading-floor dialect, risk rituals, the specific anthropology of people who say “basis points” out loud. Model the behaviour, not just the numbers.

Time SeriesJargonSimulation

TRACK 04 // CLOSE TO HOME

Campus & Commuter Life

Community college culture specifically: transfer anxiety, the parking structure, working two jobs around a 7am class, a club that meets in a borrowed room. Build for the people in the room with you.
